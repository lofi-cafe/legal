Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Lofi ("Lofi", "we", "us", or "our"), a Discord music bot, handles information when you use the bot.

2. Information We May Process

Lofi may process limited information needed to provide its features, such as:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord usernames or display names when needed to respond to commands
  • Server-specific bot settings and preferences
  • Command and technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the bot

Lofi does not intentionally collect passwords, payment card information, or the contents of private Discord conversations.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To provide Lofi's music and bot functionality
  • To remember server settings and preferences
  • To respond to commands and requests
  • To prevent abuse and protect the service
  • To diagnose problems and improve reliability

4. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Lofi, maintain server settings, prevent abuse, or satisfy legitimate operational requirements. Stored server settings may remain until they are changed, deleted, or the bot is removed from the server.

5. Data Deletion

If you want data associated with your server or use of Lofi deleted, contact us through our GitHub repository or Discord community. Please provide enough information to identify the relevant server or data.

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or legitimate operational purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Lofi operates through Discord and may use third-party infrastructure or services to host the bot, store settings, or provide music-related functionality. Such services may process limited information as necessary to provide their functions.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Lofi. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Lofi is not specifically directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including access, correction, or deletion. Contact us through GitHub or Discord to make a request.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Lofi team through:

  • GitHub
  • Discord
